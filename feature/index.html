Take it easy, Project 2000's Got You 😉

Project 2000 is a college check list and helper tool. It helps you stay organized and on track. It does this by helping you keep records of your test score, GPA, academic achievements, and extracurricular activities. Additionally, Project 2000 provides links to extra resources and information to help you stay in the know
